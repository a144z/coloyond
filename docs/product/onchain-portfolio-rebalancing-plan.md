## 1. Decision in one page

Build a **Coloyond Anchor vault program** for the complete product. Its token custody, share accounting, target weights, conditional rules, auction terms, fills, fee reserve and safety checks live in Solana accounts and instructions. Anyone may invoke an eligible transition or fill an auction. Run two independent, replaceable keeper processes for liveness; they have **no discretionary custody or rule-changing authority**. The program rechecks every condition and price on-chain. This is the strongest practical meaning of “fully on-chain” on Solana: programs execute only when a transaction invokes them, so no SDK or program can wake itself at a time or price boundary. [Solana execution model](https://solana.com/docs/core/programs/program-execution), [Solana transaction model](https://solana.com/docs/core).

Use [Pyth Core's sponsored, fixed-address SOL/USD and USDC/USD Solana push-feed accounts](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/push-feeds/solana) as the primary on-chain price source. They are listed for mainnet and Devnet with a 55-second heartbeat or 0.5% deviation update; pin and verify the full addresses and feed IDs before deployment. A permissionless [Price Pusher](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/schedule-price-updates/using-price-pusher) is the liveness backup. Use the existing [Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter Kotlin client](https://docs.solanamobile.com/android-native/using_mobile_wallet_adapter) to obtain user signatures. Implement a small TypeScript keeper/client package from the Anchor IDL. The **core rebalance settlement is a permissionless on-chain Dutch auction between the vault and external takers**; no Jupiter API, private order store, or centralized swap signer is required to move vault assets. Solvers may source the other leg from any on-chain DEX in their own atomic transaction. Jupiter Swap V2 Router `/build` is an *optional* solver convenience only after its returned instructions, CPI path, account budget and Devnet/mainnet behavior pass an integration probe. Its Meta-Aggregator `/order` path cannot be modified or composed into our vault fill. [Jupiter Swap V2](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/swap).

**Do not use Jupiter Trigger V2 for this requirement.** Its pending orders are stored off-chain and its deposit vault is a Privy-managed custodial account. Trigger V1 used on-chain PDAs but receives critical fixes only; it is not the basis of a new protocol. [Jupiter Trigger V2](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/trigger). **Do not equate an SDK's Devnet option with a functioning market, active keeper, or production security review.** This repository's [Symmetry/Jupiter probe](../integrations/2026-10-09-symmetry-jupiter-probe.md) found no Jupiter route for 40 Symmetry share mints and no funded, small vault with enabled keeper rebalancing. Symmetry V3 remains our best *reference implementation and Devnet comparison*, not the custody core for fee routing and user-authored conditional rules.

This plan **proposes a change to the current product spec**. [HANDOFF.md](../HANDOFF.md) and the [managed strategy plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-09-coloyond-managed-strategies.md) intentionally exclude our own program/keeper and keep financial actions off for their earlier hackathon release. Sprint 0 must publish a reviewed versioned product/risk amendment identifying that exception before any funded feature is represented as live. Preserve the older spec's precise card/import/privacy/recovery rules and honest manual Glider handoff while the new financial path is developed; current Android code implements probes, **not yet the complete old card journey**. No final mainnet operator or pilot-cap decision is requested in Sprint 0; those are release-gate matters.

## 2. Product contract and trust boundary

**Product choice:** a pooled, tokenized vault per *published* strategy, starting with one curated WSOL/USDC strategy. A card remains an immutable expression of a strategy configuration revision. A saved target does not silently move assets; the user explicitly selects a live vault and signs a deposit. A remix may produce a draft, and a new on-chain vault is not implied until its configuration is published. This matches the repo's existing strategy/card model and avoids one program deployment per user. Permissionless vault creation is a later factory milestone, after one vault's economics and attack surface are measured.

| On-chain and enforceable | Off-chain, replaceable and never a source of authority |
|---|---|
| Asset and share balances; target weights; policy revision; trigger predicates; price-account ownership/feed/freshness checks; auction curve and bounds; fill accounting; fee/bounty ledger; expiry and cancellation | Wallet UI, card art, RPC/indexer, alerting, keeper scheduling, solver route discovery, transaction assembly and submission |
| The vault program moves assets only under its published rules | A keeper can suggest a transition and pay a transaction fee; it cannot set a favorable price, bypass caps, or withdraw assets |

Oracle publishers and RPC operators are external dependencies even when price proofs and results are consumed on-chain. “Fully on-chain” does **not** imply guaranteed execution, exact continuous weights, no MEV, or profit. If no one submits a transaction or fills a bounded auction, the vault stays at its last on-chain state and users see a stalled status. The program keeps in-kind redemption callable under emergency pause; a frozen underlying token account or unavailable chain can still prevent actual transfer. [Solana token freeze semantics](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/freeze-account).

### End-to-end user and protocol loop

1. **Publish policy:** governance sets asset mint/feed allowlist, target weights summing to 10,000 bps, drift thresholds, max trade and loss budgets, cooldown, fee bounds and timelock. A configuration hash/revision links the vault to the card.
2. **Enter:** wallet signs a proportional basket deposit. The program transfers underlying tokens to PDA-controlled token accounts and mints SPL shares with `minSharesOut`. WSOL is the canonical SOL asset; client wrapping is explicit. The initial seed requires fresh oracle valuation, a minimum locked share amount, an approved target ratio and a disclosed seed contribution to the holder-owned automation reserve; otherwise the first rebalance cannot pass its bounty-funding guard. Subsequent proportional deposits use current backing ratios, so they do not require a spot quote or transfer value from existing holders.
3. **Observe:** anyone reads the same pinned Pyth on-chain feed accounts and invokes `observe_condition` or `start_rebalance`; a price pusher may update those feeds independently when necessary. The program checks fully verified price, feed, age and confidence, then computes value weights in integer/fixed-point math. When a rule or drift condition is satisfied, it records a deterministic, single active rebalance intent.
4. **Trigger:** after cooldown and policy checks, anyone invokes `start_rebalance`. The program freezes a bounded auction plan for overweight-to-underweight assets. A conditional price rule may first change targets by a preauthorized amount, then open the same rebalance path.
5. **Fill:** any taker supplies the deficit asset and receives the surplus asset in one Solana transaction. The program verifies fresh price, auction price, size, slippage/value loss ceiling, direction, remaining inventory and monotonic improvement toward the target before transfers. Partial fills are allowed. A solver may atomically fund its leg from a DEX, but the vault is safe even if that route fails.
6. **Settle:** explicit fill fees stay in vault-controlled accounts; an on-chain rule assigns a bounded portion to automation reserve. Claimable keeper bounties accrue only after verified useful transitions. When the target band is reached or the auction expires, anyone finalizes. State, fees, actual weights, timestamps and signatures can be independently read from Solana.
7. **Exit/recover:** a holder burns shares for a proportional, in-kind claim on all unencumbered backing, including their share of unused reserve. The program-level path needs no oracle, keeper, Jupiter share-token route, or live auction. Pending liabilities are accounted for before redemption. Emergency pause blocks new risk while preserving this path; an issuer freeze or chain outage remains an external transfer risk.

**First published vault assets:** provisionally classic SPL WSOL and USDC, conditional on mint-authority, freeze-authority, liquidity and oracle review. The older spec never mandates USDC or an issuer-free stablecoin: its illustrated assets are examples, its allocation drafts may express desired mints, and only an exact match to a verified published vault can become financial. Therefore use the old spec's full-mint, allowlist, configuration, independent entry/exit and disclosure discipline for technical asset admission; no new user policy decision about USDC is needed now. USDC carries issuer/freeze risk even though portfolio execution is on-chain; disclose and test that external-transfer limit, and never suggest the program can redeem a frozen token account. If WSOL/USDC fails a technical admission check, leave that vault unavailable and re-evaluate its asset set under a new manifest. Token-2022 extensions, unverified mints, lending, leverage, LP ranges, cross-chain routes and one-click single-token deposits add different risk models and are outside this first vault. Additional eligible assets and vaults have an explicit Sprint 7. Source mint addresses, token program IDs and full Pyth feed-account addresses/IDs must be frozen in a reviewed deployment manifest, never inferred from symbol text. [Solana token-program distinction](https://solana.com/docs/payments/how-payments-work), [Pyth feed/account validation](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/use-real-time-data/pull-integration/solana).

### Why the custom core wins this particular requirement

Symmetry is the fastest way to demonstrate **a** multi-asset on-chain vault, and we should run its Devnet lifecycle as a control experiment. It does not currently establish all three product-specific guarantees together: a verified on-chain price predicate for card-authored conditional orders, a vault-owned fee reserve with policy-limited spending, and observed keeper coverage for the chosen vault. Wrapping Symmetry shares would add a second share layer while leaving its auction and fee rules outside our control. A small, separately audited Anchor vault makes the requirement testable at one trust boundary. Reuse protocols as execution venues and design evidence; avoid importing their assumptions into our custody invariants.

## 4. On-chain contract design

**Accounts, all versioned and PDA-derived:** `Vault` (asset mints, target bps, revision, share mint, governance and pause flags); one `AssetPosition` and custody token account per asset; `OraclePolicy` (pinned canonical feed-account addresses and feed IDs, owner, max age/confidence/deviation); `ConditionalRule` (price-cross/schedule predicate, prior observed price and strictly increasing publish time, hysteresis, expiry, target revision and nonce); one active `RebalanceIntent` per vault; `Auction` (pair, snapshot, bounded price curve, start/end, remaining size); `AutomationReserve` per asset plus explicit unpaid bounty liabilities; and `GovernanceProposal` with timelock. Separate event versions permit deterministic index reconstruction. Unknown account versions fail closed.

**Instructions:** `initialize_vault`, `propose_policy`, `execute_policy_after_timelock`, `deposit_proportional`, `redeem_in_kind`, `observe_condition`, `activate_condition`, `start_rebalance`, `fill_auction`, `finish_or_expire`, `claim_bounty`, `pause_risk`, `resume_risk`, and `sync_donation` under a published rule. Each writes a sequence number so repeated keeper submissions are idempotent or reject on stale state. No manager can transfer underlying tokens to an arbitrary wallet. High-risk **configuration** changes use the program's on-chain multisig/timelock policy; emergency pause is narrower than governance. The Solana loader's separate `ProgramData` upgrade authority can bypass in-program rules, so it must be transferred to an independently enforced on-chain multisig plus delay mechanism, or revoked after the upgrade policy is settled. Its actual address and delay are launch-gate evidence. [Solana program upgrades](https://solana.com/docs/core/programs/program-deployment).

**Share invariant:** total issued shares represent a pro-rata claim on **all** unencumbered portfolio and reserve assets. Pending bounty liabilities are excluded. Vault initialization, custody-account creation and first seed deposit occur atomically, so no pre-seed donation can be mistaken for the founder's deposit. Fresh approved prices define the seed share scale and a permanently locked minimum share quantity prevents first-depositor/inflation attacks. Later deposits mint from the minimum per-asset backing ratio using vault-favoring rounding; unused inputs return in the same transaction. Redemptions burn before transferring the caller's pro-rata asset and unused-reserve portions, with explicit dust rules. Auctions do not escrow extra inventory: each fill is bounded by current custody balances and the current surplus. Proportional redemption during an auction reduces those live limits, while the frozen auction's remaining cap remains an upper bound; exit never waits for bidders. A direct token donation after initialization cannot silently change accounting: `sync_donation` credits it to holder backing under deterministic rounding, and deposits/redemptions either sync first in the same transaction or reject until synced. No float arithmetic, unchecked decimal conversion, or share calculation from target weights alone. `minSharesOut` and `minAmountsOut[]` protect the user.

**Trigger math:** normalize `value_i = token_base_units_i × Pyth_price_i × 10^(Pyth_exponent_i) / 10^(mint_decimals_i)` into one checked fixed-point USD scale, then `actual_i = value_i / Σvalue`; explicitly bound exponent, overflow and rounding. A rebalance may start only if policy is enabled, no rebalance is active, the reserve can fund the bounded bounty, the schedule is open, cooldown elapsed, and at least one asset exceeds configured drift. Use separate start and stop bands (illustrative 500 bps and 200 bps) to avoid churn. Read the **pinned fixed Pyth feed accounts** with `PriceUpdateV2::get_price_no_older_than`, which requires `Full` verification; check owner, exact feed ID, positive price, confidence and age on initial seed, condition activation, auction start and every fill. Proportional later deposits and in-kind exits use token ratios and do not need an oracle. Set max age from measured 55-second heartbeat plus jitter and acceptable adverse-selection risk; a hard-coded 30 seconds would often fail without our own pusher. Do not accept the partially verified single-transaction shortcut for funded fills. A second independent on-chain price guard is desirable only if Sprint 0 finds a sound source. Any stale, missing, wide-confidence, depegged or divergent price **blocks new risk**; it never becomes a zero or last-known executable price. A price-cross rule stores a below-threshold observation and accepts an above-threshold observation (or reverse) only when Pyth `publish_time` strictly increases, a minimum slot/time separation passes, hysteresis is satisfied, and nonce/expiry/target-change bounds remain valid. Two different Solana slots alone do not prove two oracle observations. [Pyth feed accounts](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/use-real-time-data/pull-integration/solana), [Pyth verification-level source](https://github.com/pyth-network/pyth-crosschain/blob/main/target_chains/solana/pyth_solana_receiver_sdk/src/price_update.rs), [Pyth price safety](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/best-practices).

**Auction safety:** the program snapshots eligible overweight-to-underweight pairs but checks a current price on every fill. The publicly computable Dutch price moves within a governance-set oracle-relative band over a fixed window. For every partial fill, verify both token-account deltas and that the **post-fee, post-bounty** deviation measure falls by at least a dust-adjusted minimum; cap per-fill and per-epoch size, prohibit overshoot, and limit conservative oracle-marked NAV loss including fees and bounties. Fees are taken from the incoming asset in the same fill and credited to vault-controlled ledgers. Oracle latency and adverse selection still exist at a fresh timestamp, so the band must include confidence, size and cumulative-loss limits. Expiry cannot extend its own price band; re-evaluation requires a new intent. A solver may compose a route before/after `fill_auction`, but the vault never approves a generic arbitrary program or token account. The Solana transaction is atomic for each fill, not across an entire multi-fill rebalance. [Solana atomic transactions](https://solana.com/docs/core), [Folio auction reference](https://github.com/reserve-protocol/reserve-index-dtfs-solana), [Pyth executable-price risks](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/best-practices).

**Fee and bounty economics:** do not copy YieldBasis percentages or assume Jupiter fees return to this vault. Only fees explicitly charged by `fill_auction` are protocol revenue; a disclosed seed contribution bootstraps the holder-owned reserve before any fees exist. A versioned on-chain split credits (a) unrestricted holder backing and (b) that reserve, both included in NAV; any unused reserve is part of in-kind exits. The reserve can pay only an earned, capped keeper bounty after an observed useful transition, with per-epoch spend and minimum-balance floors. Model `net benefit = fee + price improvement − adverse selection − bounty − transaction/solvency cost`. Sprint 4 must compare candidate fee/split levels under quiet, trending and volatile markets before fixing mainnet values. An auction can be uneconomic or unfilled; the reserve is not an invented yield source.

**Orders:** the initial conditional rule is a *vault-level target change* with on-chain price predicate, not a claim that any trader's external limit order will fill. **Appended Sprint 9** adds a separate user order module that escrows the user's token in an `Order` PDA and allows any solver to fill at or better than the on-chain minimum exchange rate after an on-chain Pyth trigger; include partial-fill accounting, user cancellation, expiry and permissionless reclaim. Its settlement is separate from vault shares. Only after audited composition may a fill deposit output into a vault in the same transaction. This keeps “portfolio condition” and “personal order” semantics distinct and avoids routing private orders through a custodial API.

## 5. Off-chain automation that cannot override the chain

Two independent keeper deployments, on separate RPC providers and signing keys, observe vault accounts and the pinned Pyth feed accounts; a third party can run the same open-source binary. The sponsored push feeds are the primary price-updater path; self-operated permissionless Price Pushers are backups. Since the August 2026 Pyth Core upgrade, Hermes fetching requires an API key, so distribute that off-chain availability risk across operators and never make one key an on-chain authorization requirement. Each loop derives the next valid action from chain state, simulates it, submits with bounded fees, waits for confirmed/finalized status, and re-reads the sequence number. Use account subscriptions plus periodic full reconciliation; neither WebSocket nor an indexer is the source of truth. Restart from on-chain accounts, not a private queue. Key material belongs in a dedicated keeper signer, never in the APK or repository. Keys pay fees and receive only earned bounties. A public runbook and event/account decoder let other operators compete. [Pyth push feeds](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/push-feeds/solana), [Pyth price-updater guidance](https://docs.pyth.network/price-feeds/core/use-real-time-data/pull-integration/solana).

The SDK is a **transaction builder**, not an autonomous scheduler: `observe_condition`/`activate_condition`/`start_rebalance`/`fill_auction`/`finish_or_expire` enforce eligibility. The keeper may use [Symmetry's `KeeperMonitor`](https://docs.symmetry.fi/guides/keeper) in a separate comparative Devnet pilot; its code is not silently wired to Coloyond's program. Availability target for our own Devnet pilot: with two healthy keepers and eligible price updates, 99% of triggerable transitions are submitted within five minutes; report p50/p95 delay, stale oracle incidents, failed landing, unfilled auction time and net outcome. This is an operating SLO, not a financial guarantee. If both keepers fail, the instruction remains callable by users and other solvers, and the UI shows “eligible, awaiting executor.”

## 6. Client and repo integration

Keep `apps/android` as the card product and transaction-signing client. Complete the older spec's draft/strategy/expression identities, bounded generated scene and optional caption, deterministic PNG, app-private save, strict file/link import, share and immutable remix before describing the mobile product as complete. Art and cards work without wallet or funded capability. The older `ExpressionV1`/export-v2 contract accepts only its old strategy domains; the new Coloyond vault needs a **separate versioned strategy, expression and export contract**, frozen in Sprint 0, rather than silently broadening `cb2:` or its old importer. Add read-only RPC decoders and deterministic builders for the new Anchor IDL, with generated TypeScript fixture vectors to prove Kotlin byte equality. Prefer the older spec's MWA sign-only reviewed-message byte comparison before broadcast. A sign-and-send wallet is supported for funded use only if its actual behavior can still meet the same review and durable recovery contract; otherwise keep its funded flow unavailable. Decode and display program ID, vault, mints, max input/min output, share amount, fees and policy revision before wallet handoff. Verify the returned signature, confirmed transaction message and on-chain effects before showing success. Persist a pending intent before the wallet switch and reconcile after process death; never blindly resend. Benchmark Solana v0/ALT and newly active v1 transactions against actual Android wallet/RPC support; v1 has larger messages but no ALT and different budget configuration. Keeper operations do not require the user's phone to stay open. The existing `yourdapp.com` MWA identity is a placeholder and must become an app-owned verified origin before a funded flow. [Current Android architecture](../../apps/android/README.md), [MWA guide](https://docs.solanamobile.com/android-native/using_mobile_wallet_adapter), [Solana transaction formats](https://solana.com/docs/core/transactions/versioned-transactions).

The browser UI at `/` remains a presentation prototype until it reads the same live program accounts. A `reference` design route must remain removable and nonfinancial. A card can display target configuration, observed actual weights, oracle age, share supply, NAV estimate, active/expired auction, fees, reserve and last confirmed rebalance as **separately labeled facts**. No sample balance, invented signature, stale quote or unverified keeper action may be shown as live. Strategy revision hashes remain stable for saved cards; moving to a new target creates a new financial revision, while older cards retain their historical target.

### Required test and evidence matrix

| Property | Evidence before enabling funded entry |
|---|---|
| Solvency and shares | For every state transition: total unencumbered token claims plus booked liabilities match vault-controlled balances; no user can mint shares against another user's deposit or extract more than their claim. |
| Price integrity | Wrong owner/address/feed, old or partially verified update, reused publish time, wide confidence, price divergence and USDC depeg freeze new auctions/conditional triggers; program-level exit remains callable even without a price. |
| Rebalance correctness | Every fill respects max input, min output, fee and bounded NAV loss, reduces deviation, cannot overshoot, and is atomic; partial fills and concurrent callers preserve one active intent. |
| Liveness and recovery | Two keepers, different RPCs, restarts, duplicate submissions, dropped WebSocket events, reorg/finality delay, exhausted bounty, no bidders and auction expiry all have observable, recoverable states. No unbounded loop or admin-only unsticking step. |
| Economic safety | Benchmark fees earned, value leakage, bounty paid and keeper participation under realistic liquidity; stop/cap rules dominate optimistic yield claims. |
| Client truth | Android shows actual chain account state and precise network; confirms token balance/share deltas and policy revision; wallet rejection, timeout and process death never become a false success. |
| Governance | Fee/asset/oracle policy uses in-program multisig + delay; the separate loader upgrade authority has an externally enforced on-chain delay/multisig or is revoked. Emergency actor can pause new risk but cannot seize funds; in-kind exit path is tested under pause and a token-freeze scenario is disclosed. |

## 8. Decision gates and non-negotiable exclusions

1. **Sprint 0 architecture gate:** if the pinned Pyth feed or on-chain market lacks viable freshness/liquidity, reduce the initial asset universe or stop; do not make a centralized price/route API authoritative. Verify all advertised Devnet deployments directly before coding against them. Attempt the reviewed Reserve Folio controller route and reject it only on explicit failed compatibility/trust/fee criteria.
2. **Sprint 2 economic gate:** a fill must improve target drift without exceeding conservative price/NAV loss bounds. If solvers do not appear at safe prices, do not widen bounds automatically; extend/expire, display the stall and reconsider the incentive design.
3. **Sprint 4 fee gate:** pick fee and reserve parameters from measured spread, oracle uncertainty, transaction cost and keeper competition. The previously quoted 50/50 and 70/30 examples are hypotheses, not inherited facts or defaults.
4. **Sprint 5 security gate:** no public mainnet custody without independent program audit, verified deployment, `ProgramData` authority disclosure and independently enforced upgrade controls, reproducible tests, TVL cap and emergency exit drill. A verified build proves source correspondence, not safety. [Solana verified builds](https://solana.com/docs/programs/verified-builds).
5. **Sprint 6 user gate:** a funded mainnet pilot needs explicit approval of amount/cap and real-device signing. No keeper uptime promise, principal guarantee, or “atomic whole rebalance” language.

The planned system does **not** assume a generic Solana cron, active Symmetry keepers, Jupiter Trigger V2 on-chain orders, liquid Symmetry share-token markets, Devnet support inferred from local tests, YieldBasis's leveraged economics, or automatic access to someone else's DEX fees.

## 9. Final independent recheck: stack, executable loop and changed decisions

Three read-only research tracks independently rechecked custody/oracles, execution venues, and liveness/mobile against the official sources below. The **core decision did not change**: Coloyond should own a minimal two-asset Anchor vault and permissionless bounded auction if Sprint 0 fails to prove a safer existing core with identical policy and fee control. The material implementation change is **fixed Pyth Core push-feed accounts first**, with permissionless pusher backup, instead of defaulting to ephemeral pull updates. Reserve Folio Solana is promoted to a real Sprint 0 alternative because it has an independent review; SVS-8 is demoted from a possible rebalance engine to an accounting reference. Nord's API/bridge boundary and Meteora's distinct DLMM limit-order venue are now explicit.

| Responsibility | Selected stack | What runs on-chain | What still runs off-chain |
|---|---|---|---|
| User authorization and client | Kotlin/Compose Android, MWA 2.0, exact Anchor IDL builders | Wallet-signed deposit/redemption and all vault effects | UI, transaction assembly, RPC reads, wallet handoff |
| Custody and accounting | Anchor Rust program, classic SPL Token, PDA-controlled WSOL/USDC accounts, SPL share mint | Target revision, token custody, share mint/burn, in-kind claims, pause/governance | Build, audit, monitoring; no server custody |
| Price evidence | Pyth Core fixed SOL/USD and USDC/USD feed accounts, `pyth-solana-receiver-sdk` Full verification | Feed/account/age/confidence/publish-time checks on seed, condition activation, auction start and fill | Sponsored feed updater and independently runnable Price Pusher; Hermes API keys are an availability tool, not a vault key |
| Trigger and execution | On-chain rule evaluation plus bounded Dutch auction | Drift/cross predicate, auction opening, price/size/loss checks, atomic partial fill | Independent TS keeper callers and competing solvers submit transactions; optional Jupiter Router/Manifest/Meteora route discovery |
| Economics and audit trail | Vault fee/reserve/bounty ledgers, event/account decoder | Fee capture only on our auction fill, bounded reserve spend, finalization, readable state | Indexer/dashboard and reporting may lag or fail without changing truth |
| Governance | In-program policy timelock; separate loader upgrade authority control | Configuration proposal/execution and externally enforced upgrade authority | Human multisig signers approve high-risk changes after delay |

**Concrete loop:** suppose a published WSOL/USDC vault targets 50/50. Price movement makes its on-chain marked weights 56/44. A Pyth-sponsored update or independent pusher refreshes the pinned feed accounts. A keeper, user or third party calls the vault; the program itself checks the configured drift threshold and opens an auction to sell excess WSOL for USDC. A solver sends USDC and receives bounded WSOL in one atomic fill; each fill must improve post-fee drift and obey the oracle-relative loss budget. The incoming-asset fee increases holder backing and the holder-owned automation reserve under the published split; a proven useful caller may claim a capped bounty. Anyone finalizes at the stop band or expiry. The app reads the resulting account state and transaction signatures, and a holder can burn shares for proportional assets without waiting for a solver. The 56/44 and 50/50 figures illustrate direction only; thresholds, fees and executable prices are fixed by tested on-chain policy, not this example.

**What remains unproven:** no Coloyond custody program, audit, Devnet auction liquidity, keeper uptime, Android funded-signing flow or verified upgrade authority exists yet. The native Android card journey is also unfinished beyond probes. Official documentation proves capabilities and constraints, not live integration. Sprint 0 must distinguish deployed program, SDK option, oracle freshness, actual market depth and keeper service as five separate checks. The former 13-week protocol estimate must be re-baselined for the restored card path and appended Sprints 7–9; security and liquidity gates can extend it.
