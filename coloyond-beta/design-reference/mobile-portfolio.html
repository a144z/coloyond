A focused mobile journey for the Solana hackathon: connect, own a portfolio, and let scheduled execution maintain its allocation. The Kotlin app uses MWA; this HTML is only a screen study.

Two independent vaults, one connected owner. Every number on this page is illustrative.

Monday's cut

Two tokens. Two vaults. One real unattended swap. Owner editing and in-kind exit. Cards, games, cross-chain routes, public LP shares and a second release client stay outside the deadline.

The design keeps token custody on Solana. A funded test reserve is the counterparty; MagicBlock supplies scheduled execution. Availability and fees must be demonstrated.
